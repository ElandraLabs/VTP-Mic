# Changelog

Releases are tagged `mic-vX.Y.Z`. The newest first.

## 1.0.0

First public version.

- Phone as a wireless microphone for Voice Typer Pro over Wi-Fi, with Bluetooth as a seamless backup; encrypted.
- Dictation and audio recording from the big circle, with pause and resume.
- Several phones at once; choose the dictation microphone and the button's job from the phone.
- Home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile, compact notification and lock-screen controls.
- Light, dark and system themes; Persian and English; portrait and landscape.
