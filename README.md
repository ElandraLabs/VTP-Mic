# VTP-Mic

**Turn your Android phone into a wireless microphone for [Voice Typer Pro](https://github.com/ElandraLabs/VoiceTyperPro-Updates).**
Your voice goes straight from the phone to your own computer, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, encrypted. No account, no cloud.

## How it works

1. Install VTP-Mic on the phone and open **Phone** in Voice Typer Pro's settings on the computer.
2. Scan the QR code once. From then on the two find each other by themselves.
3. Tap the big circle on the phone to dictate, or choose "Record audio" to save the sound to a file.

Features: Wi-Fi with Bluetooth as a seamless backup, several phones at once, pause and resume, a home-screen widget, a
Quick Settings tile, lock-screen controls, light and dark themes, Persian and English.
